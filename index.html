About Me

Hi! I’m Jiajun Cao (曹家骏), a Ph.D. student at the School of Computer Science, Peking University, advised by Prof. Shanghang Zhang. My research focuses on multimodal large models, world-action models, and embodied intelligence.

I received my B.Eng. in Artificial Intelligence from Xi’an Jiaotong University. I have worked on world models at the Beijing Innovation Center of Humanoid Robotics and on multimodal perception and autonomous driving at XPeng and NIO. Feel free to get in touch!

Research Interests

How can multimodal models perceive efficiently and act reliably in the physical world?

  • Embodied intelligence: connecting language reasoning, world modeling, and robotic action.
  • Efficient multimodal learning: visual knowledge distillation, token pruning, and expert specialization.
  • Vision-language-action models: perception and planning for autonomous driving and manipulation.
